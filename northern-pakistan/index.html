Smart risk placement

🗣️ Learn some Urdu in Islamabad

NUML — the obvious choice

The National University of Modern Languages (NUML), Islamabad runs Urdu courses for foreigners — short & online proficiency courses (an 8-week short course runs afternoons, ~3 hrs/day). Great to start online before you fly. numl.edu.pk/olc

Private tutors & apps

For a short trip a private tutor is most flexible — find them on TeacherOn, or use italki/Preply online. Even a few phrases — as-salamu alaikum, shukriya, kitne ka hai? — go a long way.

⚠️ Safety & season

Mid-September timing & the flights

Lovely timing — Hunza's golden autumn is beginning. The flaky mountain flight (Gilgit-in) sits at the start, where a cancellation just means driving up. The exit is the reliable direct Skardu→Lahore, with a Skardu buffer day (Day 10) in front of it — so nothing threatens your Sep 23 flight home.

Pakistan carries an overall US "Level 3 – Reconsider Travel" advisory; Gilgit-Baltistan is among the more tourist-friendly regions, and Islamabad & Lahore are the lower-risk zones. Use a reputable driver-guide, travel by day, and check your own government's advisory and insurance before booking.

Altitude: Khunjerab (~4,700 m) and the base-camp hike (~3,500–4,000 m) — nothing technical, but ascend gradually and hydrate. Modest dress for Lahore's mosques.

Practical notes

🚙 Fly the dull legs, drive the beautiful ones

A hired 4×4 with driver covers the scenic overland stretches (Hunza gorges, the Indus into Baltistan, up to Fairy Meadows). The two short flights skip the long, unremarkable hauls in and out.

✈️ Keep Day 2 flexible; hold the Skardu buffer

If the Gilgit flight is scrubbed on Day 2, start driving up instead (long, but it's early in the trip). Don't give up the Day 10 Skardu buffer — it's your insurance for the direct Lahore flight.

🎒 Packing

Layers for big day–night swings, a warm jacket even in September, proper hiking shoes for the base-camp day, sun protection, a power bank, and cash — ATMs thin out beyond Gilgit and Skardu.
