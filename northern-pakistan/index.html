Why Skardu as a base

Skardu (~2,300 m) sits in the middle of Baltistan's best: lakes, cold deserts, Shigar & Khaplu valleys, Deosai, and the Hushe trekking valleys — nearly all reachable in under 3 hrs. One comfortable base, no long transfers, and it's where your flight out leaves from.

The trek, safely

Two options for the 4-day trek: Nangma Valley ("Pakistan's Yosemite") is an easy-to-moderate, non-technical camping trek to ~4,000 m among granite towers, and Deosai Plains is the even-gentler, wildlife-rich plateau. Both far safer than the Fairy Meadows jeep track — tap the tabs below to compare.

⚠️ Safety, altitude & the trek

Altitude — the main thing to watch

Antonio goes sea-level → Skardu (Sep 15) → ~4,000 m within a few days. The Sep 16 easy day helps, but ascend gradually on the trek, hydrate hard, and this is exactly what his prescribed acetazolamide (Diamox) is for. Don't start (or continue) the trek if anyone has worsening headache, nausea or breathlessness — descending is the cure.

Book the trek operator today

Short notice: line up a Skardu guide + porters + tents + permits now, plus the Gilgit→Skardu driver and Antonio's airport pickup. Confirm it's a proper crew with warm sleeping bags (nights near freezing).

Mountain flights & the buffer

The Skardu flight can cancel for weather like the Gilgit one did — build slack around Antonio's arrival. Keep the Sep 21 buffer before flying to Lahore; it protects the way home.

Pakistan carries a US "Level 3 – Reconsider Travel" advisory; Gilgit-Baltistan is among the more tourist-friendly regions. Use a reputable driver-guide, travel by day, and check your own government's advisory and insurance.

Practical notes

🎒 Packing for the trek

Full warm + waterproof layers, hat & gloves, waterproof hiking boots, a warm sleeping bag (or confirm the operator's), trekking poles, sunglasses & strong sunscreen, headlamp, power bank, and cash — ATMs are unreliable past Skardu. Nights at camp are near freezing.

🚙 Cars & drivers

No car of your own is fine: your Skardu operator or guesthouse arranges the day-trip drivers and the trek jeeps, and a Gilgit agency handles the Gilgit→Skardu drive. Agree route, vehicle (4×4) and price up front; pay cash in PKR.
